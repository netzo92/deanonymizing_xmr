What we can and cannot determine so far

What the evidence supports

  • Describe observed ring membership, index spacing, shared candidates and same-transaction relationships.
  • Report stored deterministic resolutions under the analyzer’s assumptions, separately from model guesses.
  • Measure feature behavior and retrospective agreement on declared, frozen historical samples.
  • Record transactions visible to our public-RPC observer, polling gaps and later block matches.
  • Describe structural differences between sampled protocol eras, while showing missing coverage.

What remains unestablished

  • Sender identities, IP addresses, receiving addresses or common wallet ownership.
  • The actual spend in every ring, or the correctness of an unverified model choice.
  • General accuracy on modern Monero from the early historical sample.
  • Calibrated confidence, independent ground truth or improved forward confirmation forecasts.
  • A complete network pool census or global arrival time from one restricted node.

These are the current scope limits. Each experiment below records its own population, evidence date and remaining work.
